%% A right-to-left document under PDF/UA-2: a Hebrew example, an english
%% example mirrored into it, and an English example glossed in Hebrew --
%% every direction mechanism of rtl-main and rtl-gloss at once, validated
%% by veraPDF under LuaLaTeX (under XeLaTeX a tagged right-to-left
%% document fails 8.2.2 with no linguexx loaded at all -- bidi's matter, see
%% the assertion).  And the tree must READ right, on both: XeTeX writes a right-to-left
%% word's glyphs in visual order, so the Hebrew glosses of the English grid
%% carry their logical text (\__lx_gl_if_dircells:, the cell layers of
%% \GlossRTL used in the left-to-right box too); without it a screen reader
%% is handed "דליה" for "הילד", and nothing on the page shows it.
%% Last, an ordinary Hebrew gloss with a judgment, in the Hebrew document.
%% Under XeLaTeX its marked content sits in the paragraph's R segment,
%% which reverses the operators with the glyphs: they must still balance
%% (the check every build gets, run_case).  An attempt to give its words
%% L segments of their own left one EMC with no sequence open and the
%% last sequence never closed, and veraPDF passed it.
\input{_preamble-rtlmain-ua}
\ex. PLAINREF הילד.
\a. SUBONE אכל.
\b. *SUBTWO את.
\z.
