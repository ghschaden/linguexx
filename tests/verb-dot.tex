%% The other half of the \verb matrix: in the DOT syntax it cannot work.
%%
%% A dot-syntax body is collected token by token before it is typeset --
%% which is exactly what lets a blank line end it and \z. pop a level -- so
%% by the time the body is set, its characters have been read with the
%% catcodes in force at the \ex., and \verb, which has to change those
%% catcodes WHILE reading, finds nothing left to protect.  The failure is
%% TeX's, not the package's: the "_" of the payload reaches the typesetter
%% as a subscript, which is "Missing $ inserted" on all three engines.
%%
%% This is documented as inherent to a blank-line-delimited body rather
%% than a defect (linguex grabs at \par and has it too), so the error is
%% the specification.  Pinning it means a change that made \verb work here
%% would have to be a decision -- the case turns green-by-accident into a
%% visible failure ("expected the error ...; got a clean compile").
%% The working half is verb.tex.
