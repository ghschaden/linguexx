%% Every name langsci-gb4e defines still exists under [langsci].

%% Twenty-five of them are provided -- the criterion is what WORKS upstream,
%% not what upstream's two documents describe, and the .sty records why --
%% and the remaining two, xlistabr and qlist, are defined to complain,
%% because neither has ever worked upstream and there is no behaviour to
%% reproduce.  Either way the name is defined, which is what this case
%% checks: a document being ported must never meet "Undefined control
%% sequence" on a name langsci-gb4e has.
%%
%% Existence is all this case can check, and it is worth checking on its
%% own: what the provided ones DO is asserted by langsci.tex,
%% langsci-lists.tex, langsci-extra.tex and langsci-options.tex, and what
%% the two refusals do by langsci-retired.tex.  A name dropped in a refactor
%% shows up here and nowhere else.
